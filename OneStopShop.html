Prototype Overview

Unifying fragmented shopping journeys

A PM-led concept exploring how to consolidate cross-vertical shopping into a single, low-friction mobile experience — eliminating the need for separate grocery, electronics, and fashion apps.

Cross-vertical catalog

A unified taxonomy spanning 6 product domains, surfaced through one search bar and one cart.

Daily deals engine

Time-bound personalised offers driven by category affinity + recency.

One wallet, one loyalty

Earn and redeem points across every category — no separate programmes to track.

Product analytics ready

Event taxonomy aligned with MixPanel / Heap / Pendo for funnel and retention tracking.

PM lens

"Users don't think in app silos — they think in shopping missions. The job-to-be-done is 'get me what I need today', not 'open the right vertical app'."
